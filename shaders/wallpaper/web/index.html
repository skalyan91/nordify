Wallpaper Ricer (Wallricer)

Turns a photo into a wallpaper: crops it to the size you want, softly blurs the background the way a camera lens would, and recolours everything to match a palette like Nord or Gruvbox. Blurring and cropping around the subject need a sense of depth — upload a depth map below, or let your browser work it out automatically. See README.md for the technical details.

1. Upload

Downloads two AI models the first time — roughly 70–100MB total. Your browser keeps them cached after that, and your photo is never sent anywhere; everything runs on your own device. The depth model looks at your photo in close-up passes as well as all at once, to catch fine detail a single pass would miss — thorough, but slow: a few seconds for a small photo, several minutes for a large one (about 5 minutes on a 1600×821 photo in testing).

Choose an image to begin (depth map + segmentation map are optional).
2. Filters (before cropping, live)

Applied to the original source image (never to the crop/focus decisions above) — a few ms per change, live enough to drag the night-amount slider directly. Every colour scheme's shaders are precompiled at page load, so switching applies instantly.

3. Focus (live)

Click the image to focus at that point's own depth. Needs a depth map and segmentation map (see Upload above).

4. Export

Only whichever of crop/focus, night colouring, and palette mixing are currently active — compiled specifically for the selected palette.
